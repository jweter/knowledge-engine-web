Ask Knowledge Engine

Ask starts with the indexed evidence for the question you actually typed. When that evidence is thin and Research is available, Knowledge Engine automatically broadens the literature search, acquires accessible sources, grounds new Evidence Records, re-runs the question, and releases only an answer that passes the verification gates. Sources stay visible for inspection.
