Roadmap

What's shipped, what's next, and one clearly-labeled preview of a future feature that does not exist yet.

Shipped

  • knowledge-engine-core: scientific-literature discovery, licensing checks, PDF acquisition, evidence extraction and review, and a knowledge graph (claims, PICO-resolved concepts, relationship and citation edges) -- all offline and human-auditable.
  • knowledge-engine-web (this site): a read-only view of that graph -- Graph Summary, Claims, Unconfirmed Claims, Relationship Candidates, paper detail, and now these Reports -- plus a password-gated alpha deployment for testing hosting, browsers, and real-world latency outside a local machine.
  • knowledge-engine-ai: a first Retrieval Intelligence slice (ke-ai ask) -- a natural-language question to ranked, source-linked evidence, no synthesis yet.

Near-term

  • Relationship Record rendering on this site (still needs its own design pass).
  • A real API boundary between this site and core, so the alpha can serve current data instead of a point-in-time snapshot that has to be manually refreshed and redeployed.
  • Growing the corpus and relationship-edge coverage beyond today's GLP-1 vertical slice.

Longer-term

knowledge-engine-ai is designed to add judgment in stages, each one only after the stage before it is real and verified against actual data -- never guessed ahead of what core can already support:

  • Retrieval Intelligence (started) -- question to ranked evidence, no synthesis.
  • Evidence Intelligence -- structured comparison across multiple evidence records for one question.
  • Analytical Intelligence -- weighing evidence quality, direction, and disagreement.
  • Discovery Intelligence -- surfacing open questions the corpus doesn't answer yet.

The concept preview below shows what the far end of that road could eventually look like -- not a commitment to a specific design, and not built yet.
