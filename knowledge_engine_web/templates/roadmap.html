Roadmap

What's shipped, what's next, and one clearly-labeled preview of a future feature that does not exist yet.

Shipped

  • knowledge-engine-core: scientific-literature discovery, licensing checks, PDF acquisition, evidence extraction and review, and a knowledge graph (claims, PICO-resolved concepts, relationship and citation edges) -- all offline and human-auditable.
  • knowledge-engine-web (this site): a read-only view of that graph -- Graph Summary, Claims, Unconfirmed Claims, Relationship Candidates, paper detail, Evidence and Relationship Record provenance, Evidence Intelligence, the corpus dashboard, and Reports -- plus a password-gated alpha deployment for testing hosting, browsers, and real-world latency outside a local machine.
  • knowledge-engine-ai: Retrieval Intelligence plus optional, local, citation-required synthesis (ke-ai ask --synthesize). This site exposes the same opt-in synthesis on /ask when a local model is configured.

Current Project Path

  1. One coherent public journey. Connect the public showcase to this live laboratory, keep status language and data revisions current, and guide a visitor from the mission to one real evidence-backed example.
  2. Measured Ask quality. Establish golden research questions and make source-linked evidence outrank incidental keyword mentions.
  3. One complete evidence map. Deepen the GLP-1/body-weight question across key studies, limitations, population differences, contradictions, citations, and reviewed relationships before adding more breadth.
  4. Evidence and Analytical Intelligence. Build structured cross-study comparison and deterministic statistical checks before broader AI narration. Keep Evidence Quality, Consensus, and Claim Confidence separate and inspectable.
  5. Persistent-host migration. Replace snapshot reads with a small read-only core API only after the documented operator, security, data publication, and consumer-parity trigger is met.

Longer-term

knowledge-engine-ai is designed to add judgment in stages, each one only after the stage before it is real and verified against actual data -- never guessed ahead of what core can already support:

  • Retrieval Intelligence (shipped, evaluation next) -- question to ranked evidence, with optional grounded narration.
  • Evidence Intelligence (foundations shipped) -- deterministic per-claim values today; structured comparison across multiple records next.
  • Analytical Intelligence -- weighing evidence quality, direction, and disagreement.
  • Discovery Intelligence -- surfacing open questions the corpus doesn't answer yet.

The concept preview below shows what the far end of that road could eventually look like -- not a commitment to a specific design, and not built yet.
