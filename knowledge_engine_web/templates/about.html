About Knowledge Engine

Knowledge Engine renders what knowledge-engine-core ("core") has already validated -- for a person, in a browser, instead of a terminal. This site adds no synthesis, no confidence rating, and no judgment about what a claim or relationship means.

The seam

core locates, validates, and persists evidence. It never decides what that evidence means for a person's actual question. This site holds the exact same boundary: it never sets or infers a research question, an evidence direction, or any confidence rating beyond what core already stores as free-text notes. That judgment belongs to a future, separate reasoning layer, not this one.

Concretely: every number and label on this site traces back to an actual row in core's database or an actual field in a validated evidence record -- never a value invented to make a page look more complete.

What "alpha" means here

This deployment exists to test hosting, browsers, and real-world latency outside a local machine -- not to announce a finished product. It serves a point-in-time snapshot of the corpus, not a live connection to core. Expect gaps, rough edges, and pages with very little data; the underlying corpus is still small and growing.

Scope today

The current corpus focuses on GLP-1 receptor agonists and weight loss, chosen as a bounded, well-documented vertical slice while the underlying evidence pipeline was built. See the graph summary for its current real size.

Source and feedback

This project is open source: knowledge-engine-core (evidence ingestion and validation), knowledge-engine-web (this site), and knowledge-engine-ai (the future judgment layer). Feedback and issue reports are welcome on any of those repositories.
