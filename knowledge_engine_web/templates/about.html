About Knowledge Engine

Knowledge Engine renders what knowledge-engine-core ("core") has already validated -- for a person, in a browser, instead of a terminal. It displays stored evidence and reviewed relationships, plus clearly labeled deterministic Evidence Intelligence. Optional AI narration, when configured and explicitly requested, is labeled and never treated as new evidence or scientific review.

For the project's mission, principles, and architecture, see the Knowledge Engine showcase site. This alpha is the real, working system that page describes -- not a mockup.

The seam

core locates, validates, and persists evidence. It never decides what that evidence means for a person's actual question. This site holds the exact same boundary: it never sets or infers a research question or an evidence direction. Deterministic scores summarize recorded fields; they do not decide truth or replace scientific judgment.

Stored values trace to core's database or evidence records. Computed values identify their deterministic method, relationships identify how they were classified (grounding-verified by default, or hand-authored) rather than asserting it silently, and illustrative material is labeled.

What "alpha" means here

This deployment exists to test hosting, browsers, and real-world latency outside a local machine -- not to announce a finished product. It serves a point-in-time snapshot of the corpus, not a live connection to core. Expect gaps, rough edges, and pages with very little data; the underlying corpus is still small and growing.

Scope today

The current corpus focuses on GLP-1 receptor agonists and weight loss, chosen as a bounded, well-documented vertical slice while the underlying evidence pipeline was built. See the graph summary for its current real size.

Source and feedback

This project is open source: knowledge-engine-core (evidence ingestion and validation), knowledge-engine-web (this site), and knowledge-engine-ai (the future judgment layer). Feedback and issue reports are welcome on any of those repositories.
